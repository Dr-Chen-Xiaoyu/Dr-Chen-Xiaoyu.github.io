## Abstract summary

The study used resting-state functional connectivity to build individual models of how focal stimulation affects distant brain regions. Simulations in two Parkinson’s disease cohorts ranked basal ganglia targets highly, with different targets emerging across participants. In the local cohort, the modelled rank of the subthalamic nucleus was associated with motor symptom severity.
