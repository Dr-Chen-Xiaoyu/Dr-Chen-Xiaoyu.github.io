## Abstract summary

In macaques under anesthesia, the study paired intralaminar thalamic stimulation with simultaneous EEG and fMRI to map a network associated with rapid arousal during slow-wave activity. Gene-expression analyses showed enrichment for potassium-channel genes and several cellular signatures in that network. Comparison with human expression data identified molecular and cellular features shared across primates.
