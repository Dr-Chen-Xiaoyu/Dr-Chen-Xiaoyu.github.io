## Abstract summary

This preprint introduces StaGIC, a staged gradient-based method for fitting whole-cortex models to resting-state functional connectivity and its temporal fluctuations. Synthetic experiments evaluated recovery of regional and interregional model parameters. In human data, the inferred regional parameters followed cortical hierarchy. In macaque and marmoset datasets, the strongest gains in agreement with tracer measurements were observed for long-range connections.
