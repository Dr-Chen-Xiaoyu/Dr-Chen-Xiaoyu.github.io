First published online on October 26, 2021, and assigned to the February 2022 issue.

## Abstract summary

A retrospective analysis of three resting-state fMRI cohorts identified fronto-subcortical functional connections associated with obsessive-compulsive symptom severity. Both deep brain stimulation and anterior capsulotomy were associated with stronger communication among cortical regions and weaker communication between cortical and subcortical regions. Communication in distinct prefrontal areas before treatment was linked to subsequent changes in mood or anxiety symptoms.
