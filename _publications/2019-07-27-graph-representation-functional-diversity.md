## Abstract summary

The study introduces the neighbor dispersion index, a graph measure of how dissimilar a brain region’s immediate neighbors are. Across two resting-state fMRI datasets, the index was high in frontoparietal and salience regions and low in primary sensory and motor regions. Neighbor dispersion in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex was also associated with individual differences in fluid intelligence.
