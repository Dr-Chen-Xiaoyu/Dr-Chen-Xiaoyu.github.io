## Abstract summary

An individualized model was evaluated retrospectively using resting-state fMRI collected with DBS on and off in 11 participants with obsessive-compulsive disorder and 11 with major depressive disorder. In exploratory analyses, the simulated changes mainly involved frontal–subcortical connections in the first cohort and default-mode network hubs in the second. Clinical predictions from leave-one-out validation correlated with observed outcomes in both cohorts.
