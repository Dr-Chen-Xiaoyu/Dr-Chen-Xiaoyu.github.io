## Abstract summary

The model couples a sparse representation inspired by the cerebellar granular layer with a classifier trained through error feedback inspired by Purkinje-cell learning. On Fashion-MNIST it achieved accuracy comparable to a backpropagation-trained network with one hidden layer and ran faster in the reported benchmarks. Cart-pole balancing and simulated robotic reaching demonstrated proof-of-principle closed-loop control.
