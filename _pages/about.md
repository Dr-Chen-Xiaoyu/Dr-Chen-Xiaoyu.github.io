I got my Ph.D. from Institute of Neuroscience (ION), Chinese Academy of Sciences. Now, I am a Postdoc in Lab of Computational Neuroscience, Institute of Natural Sciences (INS) and School of Mathematical Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My current supervisors are Prof. Douglas Zhou and Prof. Songting Li. 

Research topic
======
Building on 12 years of research experience in brain-wide data analysis, large-scale biophysical modelling and training, I am pursuing an ambitious research goal at the intersection between deep learning and neuroscience: **developing a biophysically grounded foundation model of human brain.** My overarching research goal is to leverage deep learning to train a unified, brain-wide foundation model that integrates biophysical principles and connectomic constraints, reproduces multimodal neural data, including fMRI, EEG and intracranial EEG (iEEG), and captures fundamental dynamical properties of the brain, such as signal propagation, cortical hierarchies and criticality. Ultimately, I envision this engineering effort will advance our mechanistic understanding of large-scale brain functions (e.g., working memory and attention), guide the design of targeted brain stimulation strategies and inspire the development of next-generation AI systems.
